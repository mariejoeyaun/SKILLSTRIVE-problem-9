# SKILLSTRIVE-problem-9

Problem Analysis

<br> Nina is 24 and learning JavaScript after changing careers. She feels like she’s not improving because she can’t see her progress, which makes her think about quitting. The main problem is that she doesn’t have a clear way to track her study hours. The solution should help her see how much time she has already spent, how close she is to her goal, and give her motivation to keep going.


<br> Features

<br> - Add skills with a name and target hours
<br> - Log study hours for each skill
<br> - See progress in cards with totals, percentages, and remaining hours
<br> - Visual progress bars that grow as hours are added
<br> - Responsive design that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
